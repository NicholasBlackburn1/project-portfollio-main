In the world of software engineering, where technology and creativity intersect, you'll find me—a trans woman, a passionate software engineer, and a proud member of the furry community. My life and work are driven by a deep love for technology, particularly in making hardware and software work seamlessly together, and a desire to push the boundaries of what's possible.

### Merging Passion with Technology

My fascination with technology began early, eventually growing into a full-blown passion for AI, machine learning, and hardware integration. The thrill of seeing a project come to life—whether it's a custom AI model, a sophisticated security system, or an innovative tool—never gets old. Each project is a testament to my dedication to creating functional and imaginative solutions that make a difference.

### The Furry Engineer's Creative Spark

Being a furry is an integral part of who I am, and it inspires the creativity I bring to my projects. I see technology as more than just a tool; it's a canvas for innovation. By blending functionality with a touch of imagination, I strive to create experiences that are not only efficient but also engaging and fun.

### A Commitment to Ethical Innovation

Ethics are at the heart of my work. I believe that technology should improve lives while respecting privacy, security, and the dignity of users. My commitment to ethical innovation drives me to create systems that protect users—especially the vulnerable—while exploring the frontiers of AI and machine learning.

### A Personal Journey of Growth

My journey has been one of continuous growth, both personally and professionally. As a trans woman, I've navigated significant challenges, from transitioning to overcoming past trauma. These experiences have shaped me into a compassionate, resilient individual who values genuine connections and strives to create a more inclusive world through technology.

Whether you're here to explore my projects, learn about the intersection of creativity and technology, or simply connect with someone who shares your passions, I'm glad you stopped by. Let's explore the endless possibilities of what we can achieve together.
